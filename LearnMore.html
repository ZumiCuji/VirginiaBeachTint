The Technical Details

Why coastal crawlspaces need special care

Between the humidity, the tides, and a high water table, Hampton Roads crawlspaces stay damp longer than most.

01 / Humidity

Summer air carries moisture into the crawlspace, hitting cooler wood and feeding mold.

02 / Water Table

Much of Hampton Roads sits near sea level. Water has nowhere to go but up.

03 / Structure

Moisture is slow, quiet, and cumulative causing wood rot and rusted ductwork over years.

Ground Cover vs. Full Encapsulation

Ground Cover (Most Popular)

  • Traps water vapor in the soil.
  • Maintains ventilation via existing vents.
  • Highly affordable ($500 - $2,500).
  • Ideal for 90% of local homes.

Full Encapsulation (Premium)

  • Completely seals out outdoor air.
  • Relies on a 24/7 dehumidifier.
  • High investment ($5,000 - $15,000+).
  • Ideal for severe standing water or chronic rot.
