Who is Cuji Crawl Space Care?

I built this company on a simple principle: you shouldn't have to choose between a damp crawlspace and a high-pressure sales pitch.

Local service without the franchise pricing.

Living in coastal Virginia, I've seen firsthand how a damp crawlspace can quietly impact a home's foundation and indoor air quality. I've also seen corporate franchises charge up to $15,000 for massive encapsulation packages that most homes don't even need.

I started Cuji Crawl Space Care to offer a straightforward, local alternative. We focus on exactly what works—proper soil-sealed vapor barriers and functional sump pump systems—saving you thousands of dollars while protecting your home.
