Nice to meet you

Honest, Local alternative for Hampton Roads

I started Cuji Crawl Space Care to deliver a transparent, direct alternative to the high-pressure $15,000 corporate franchise models.

Living and working in coastal Virginia, I've seen firsthand how our unique regional geography—characterized by sandy, moisture-retaining soils, a remarkably high water table, and heavy seasonal humidity—can silently damage crawlspaces. But I’ve also seen firsthand how the big structural franchises take advantage of this reality.

I built this company with a simple, contrasting principle: Do only what is necessary, do it cleanly, and charge fairly. For 90% of local homes with damp crawlspaces, you do not need expensive structural encapsulation, air-locking machinery, or active permanent dehumidifiers. A heavy-duty, professionally sealed 6 mil vapor barrier paired with a cleanly maintained sump pump is all it takes to keep your joists secure.

Why work with an independent owner?

  • Zero Sales Pressure: I am here to diagnose and resolve your crawlspace moisture, not hit a quarterly franchise sales target.
  • Direct Accountability: When you call or email, you talk directly to me. I’m also the specialist who inspects and works under your home.
  • Premium Local Materials: We use industry-standard 6 mil soil barriers, tightly stapled and sealed along structural piers. We also offer thicker upgrades if desired.
  • Fully Insured & Local: We reside right here in Hampton Roads. We are licensed and insured.
