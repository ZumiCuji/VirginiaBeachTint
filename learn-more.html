Why this matters here

Coastal soil keeps the water table close.

Between the humidity, the tides, and a high water table, Hampton Roads crawlspaces stay damp longer than most. Left alone, that moisture works its way into the floor joists above it.

01 / HUMIDITY

Warm, humid air condenses on cool framing

Summer air carries moisture into the crawlspace, where it hits cooler wood and settles — feeding mold, mildew, and musty odors upstairs.

02 / WATER TABLE

Groundwater sits close to the surface

Much of Hampton Roads sits near sea level. After heavy rain or a high tide, that water has nowhere to go but up into an open crawlspace.

03 / STRUCTURE

Moisture is slow, quiet, and cumulative

Wood rot, rusted ductwork, and pest activity build up unseen for years. Most homeowners find out during a home sale — or too late.

Know Your Options

Ground Cover vs. Full Encapsulation

We believe in giving you the protection your home actually needs without selling you an overpriced system. Here is how the two approaches stack up.

Ground Cover Vapor Barrier Most Popular

The standard, highly effective method for most crawlspaces. We cover 100% of the soil with a heavy-duty, sealed plastic barrier to block up to 90% of ground moisture from evaporating into your floor joists.

  • Blocks Ground Moisture: Traps water vapor directly in the soil.
  • Maintains Ventilation: Works with existing crawlspace foundation vents.
  • Highly Affordable: Typically costs $500 - $2,500 depending on crawlspace size.
  • Ideal For: 90% of local homes with normal humidity and ventilation.

Full Encapsulation Premium

A complete sealing process. Foundation vents are bricked up and sealed, walls are fully lined, and a dedicated crawlspace dehumidifier is installed to run 24/7. Highly effective, but much more expensive.

  • Total Isolation: Completely seals out the outdoor air.
  • Mechanical Control: Relies on a dehumidifier to actively pull moisture out.
  • High Investment: Costs range from $5,000 to $15,000+ with ongoing electrical/maintenance fees.
  • Ideal For: Homes with severe standing water, chronic rot, or below-grade basements.
