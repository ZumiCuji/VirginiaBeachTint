What we do

Four services, one goal: a dry crawlspace

Choose individual services or combine them for complete moisture and debris control.

Crawlspace Inspection

A thorough, professional walkthrough covering moisture levels, structural wood condition, mold, pests, standing water, and existing vapor barrier health — with full photo evidence.

Report delivered same day

Sump Pump Check

We manually test the float switch, check electrical power delivery at the GFCI outlet, and clear out mud or debris clogging the basin or discharge lines.

Test, GFCI check & debris removal

Crawlspace Clean-Out

Ripping out and hauling away old water-damaged vapor barriers, fallen insulation, organic matter, or construction debris to prepare the space for clean air.

Free on-site estimate

Moisture Barrier Installation

Heavy-duty 6 mil vapor barrier sealed to piers, walls, and seams — cutting off ground moisture at the source and giving the crawlspace a cleaner, drier finish.

6 mil, seams sealed
